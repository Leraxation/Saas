    How the budget is calculated

    Each plan line is costed month by month. Heads in month m = current HC + new hires (from their start month) − exits (after their last month). Basic and allowances rise by the increment from its effective month. Overtime is a % of basic per department; bonus accrues as n months of basic. Social protection applies to Omani and expatriate staff at their own rates, on basic or gross pay. End-of-service accrues on expatriate basic. Air tickets apply to expatriates; medical and training apply per head per year. Recruitment cost is charged in each new hire's start month. Contingency is a % on each department's total. The run-rate is today's headcount at today's pay for 12 months with no increment, hires or exits; the bridge shows what each change adds.

    Import your data

    Excel (.xlsx) or CSV. Employee-level or summarised files both work.

    Drop a file here, or

    Columns recognised (header names are matched loosely): Department, Position (or Grade / Job title), Nationality, Current HC, New hires, Start month, Exits, Last month, Basic, Allowances. Employee-level export from your HR system: leave out “Current HC”. Each row counts as one person, and rows are grouped by department, position and nationality with average pay. Nationality accepts Omani / O / OM / Local; anything else is treated as Expatriate. Months accept 1–12 or Jan–Dec.
