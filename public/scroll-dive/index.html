Chapter 02

Frames, not video

A <video> element cannot be seeked accurately enough for scrubbing — browsers snap to keyframes and stall. Painting decoded stills onto a canvas gives exact, frame-perfect control at any scroll speed.

Chapter 05

Drop in your own footage

Point ffmpeg at a clip, list the shots in CONFIG, and give each one a weight. The track height follows the same ratio. 

ffmpeg -i clip.mp4 -vf "fps=12,scale=1600:-2" \
  -c:v libwebp -quality 76 frames/frame_%04d.webp
