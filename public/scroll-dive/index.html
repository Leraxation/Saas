Chapter 02

Frames, not video

A <video> element cannot be seeked accurately enough for scrubbing — browsers snap to keyframes and stall. Painting decoded JPEGs onto a canvas gives you exact, frame-perfect control at any scroll speed.

  • 32frames in this demo
  • 400vhscroll track
  • 1second of scrub easing

Chapter 03

Cover, not squash

The canvas keeps the source resolution as its backing store and is stretched over the viewport with object-fit: cover. The composition fills any window, on any aspect ratio, without a single redraw on resize.

Chapter 04

Drop in your own sequence

Export your video to numbered JPEGs, point CONFIG in main.js at the folder, and set the frame count. A 6 second clip at 24 fps is 144 files — that is all this needs to become your own. 

ffmpeg -i clip.mp4 -vf fps=24 \
  -q:v 4 frames/frame_%04d.jpg
