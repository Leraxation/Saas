Loyalty

Sindbad

The frequent flyer programme of Oman Air. Earn on every flight, spend across the network and with partners.

How it works

Earn, then spend

  • Earn on Oman AirMiles on every fare, scaled by cabin and booking class
  • Earn with partnersPartner airlines, hotels, car hire and banks
  • Award seatsSpend miles on flights across the network
  • UpgradesMove up a cabin on eligible fares
  • ExtrasAdditional baggage and lounge access
  • Family poolingCombine miles across a household

Tiers

Four levels of recognition

  • BlueEntry tier — start earning from your first flight
  • SilverPriority check-in and extra baggage allowance
  • GoldLounge access and guaranteed Economy seats
  • PlatinumHighest tier — guaranteed premium seats and upgrades
