    How the budget is calculated

    With an employee file loaded, every employee is costed individually at their own basic and allowances, from January until their planned exit month. Without one, each plan line is costed at its average pay. New hires join from their start month at the line's new-hire pay. Other exits (unnamed attrition) leave after their last month at the line's average pay. Basic and allowances rise by the increment from its effective month; an employee's personal increment overrides the company rate. Overtime is a % of basic per department; bonus accrues as n months of basic. Social protection applies to Omani and expatriate staff at their own rates, on basic or gross pay. End-of-service accrues on expatriate basic. Air tickets apply to expatriates; medical and training apply per head per year. Recruitment cost is charged in each new hire's start month. Contingency is a % on each department's total. The run-rate is today's staff at today's pay for 12 months with no increment, hires or exits; the bridge shows what each change adds.
